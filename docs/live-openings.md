## What the market looks like today

1. **ChristianaCare is the standout employer** for this profile — three live non-research roles with published bands, bachelor's-preferred language, and a real CHW ladder. If she applies to one place, it's ChristianaCare.
2. **The best-paying non-research role in the region is a care-coordination job, not an analyst job.** LCH's MAT Coordinator posts $90–100k against a bachelor's requirement. That's an outlier, but it shows the ceiling on coordination work is higher than the coordinator titles suggest.
3. **"Population health" is almost never the job title.** The same work posts as *care coordinator, patient navigator, outreach coordinator, service coordinator, healthcare coordinator, care navigator, community educator, health coach, program liaison.* Searching the degree name returns nearly nothing; searching the job's real name returns dozens.
4. **The trap in the "community health worker" search:** roughly 7 of 9 results are housing coordinators, employment specialists, recovery coaches, and direct support professionals. These are social-services jobs that pay less and don't build health experience. She should ignore them.
5. **Volume expectation:** ~40 applications → 3–5 interviews. Silence is not a signal about her candidacy; it's the base rate.
