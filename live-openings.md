## What the live market actually looks like today (important)

A few honest observations from pulling ~120 results across both corridors:

1. **Chester County Health Department had zero openings relevant to her today.** The county's entire current listing was courts, 911 dispatch, accounting, and probation. Their health-department roles post in bursts — set the alert, don't wait on it. The adjacent **Montgomery County** TB Control posting (#6) is the live public-health job in the region right now.
2. **WellSpan (York's biggest employer) had essentially nothing for her.** Their live listings were dominated by nursing, behavioral-health clinical, and facilities roles. Corridor B's real opportunities today are **Hershey (#13)**, **Lancaster (#14, #15)**, and the smaller York nonprofits — not WellSpan.
3. **ChristianaCare is the standout employer in the whole region** for this profile: three live roles with published bands, bachelor's-preferred language, and a genuine CHW career ladder. If she applies to one place, it's ChristianaCare.
4. **The volume is thinner than the "1,000 jobs" headlines suggest.** Indeed's West Chester search for "community health worker" returned mostly housing coordinators, employment specialists, and recovery coaches — adjacent social-services work, not health roles. Of 9 top results, roughly 1–2 were genuine fits. She should expect to apply to 40–60 postings to land 3–5 interviews, and should not read silence as a signal about her candidacy.
5. **Contract roles are her fastest path into clinical research.** #8 (Medix, West Chester) and #21 (DM Clinical Research) hire quickly and convert to permanent at a high rate. Given she wants a job *now*, a 6-month contract CRC role at $27/hr beats waiting three months for a health-system req to close.
